Extract your FM26 save. Scout it like a moneyball analyst.

FM26 Scout pulls every player's data out of your Football Manager 26 save and opens it as a scouting dashboard. Run moneyball on pure match stats, or just find the best players for your team.

Download for Windows Unzip, then run FM26-Scout.exe Download for Mac Apple Silicon (M1 or later)

Free · No account · Your save stays on your computer

Three ways to find your next signing

Switch between them in one click, inside the app. Same save, three different lenses.

Everything the save knows

All the data, including each player's current and potential ability (CA and PA). The complete picture when you want to dig deep.

Only what a manager can see

No CA or PA, no hidden attributes, no personality numbers. Scout with the same information the game gives you.

Only what happened on the pitch

No attributes at all. A performance score and per-90 match stats, set against fee and wage. Find the players whose output beats their price.

What you can do with it

Search the whole save

Every player in your save, men and women, from every nation. Stack filters on age, position, value, wage, contract and attributes, then save the searches you use again.

Spot a bargain

Deal Finder compares a player's real fee and wage with an estimate of the fair price, so the good deals rise to the top.

Know where your squad is thin

My Club reads your formation from your recent matches, scores every slot, ranks your players position by position and lists the best-value targets for your weakest spots.

Check an offer before you send it

Enter a fee and a wage. Offers gives you a verdict, an estimate of the chance the club accepts, and shows how the fair price is built.

Open any player

One card with ability, standout attributes and traits, contract, and price against fair price. Star players to build shortlists and export any search to CSV.

From save file to scouting dashboard in a few minutes

  1. Download and open

    Unzip the file and double-click FM26 Scout. Your browser opens on its own.

  2. Pick your save

    FM26 Scout lists the Football Manager 26 saves it finds. Click one and wait about a minute while it extracts the data.

  3. Start scouting

    Filter, compare, shortlist. Close the tab when you are done and the app closes with it.

Your save stays yours

FM26 Scout runs on your own computer. The save is read locally and nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no account, no tracking and no subscription.

The first launch asks for one extra click

FM26 Scout is not code-signed yet, so your computer asks you to confirm before opening it. You only do this once. The code is public on GitHub if you want to check what it does.

On Mac

  1. Double-click FM26 Scout. macOS says it can't open it.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm.

Works on Apple Silicon Macs only. Intel Macs are not supported.

On Windows

  1. Unzip the folder first. Do not run it from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click FM26-Scout.exe. If Windows protects your PC, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
