The app

The ScoutSheet app runs on your computer. Your save and your data never leave it. At launch, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. Nothing about you or your save is sent, and you can turn the check off in Settings.

Usage stats, only if you say yes. At first launch the app asks once whether it may share usage stats. If you agree, it counts per day which features you use (modes, searches, player profiles, exports, My Club, shortlist), how long the app is on screen, the app version, your operating system and the type of any error, under a random ID created on your computer. It never sends your save, file names, players, clubs or leagues. Counts wait on your computer while you are offline, go to this site (hosted by Cloudflare) when you are back online, and are deleted after 14 months. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). Turn it off anytime in Settings → Privacy: the app then deletes its local counts and asks this site to delete everything sent under your ID.

This website

The site has no account, no form and no ads. It measures visits in two ways:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, for everyone. It sets no cookies and does not identify you: it counts page views, the referring site, the country and the type of device.
  • Our own count, for everyone. No cookie and no identifier, and your IP address is not kept: the site only adds to daily totals per page of how long pages stay on screen and how the cookie banner is answered.
  • Google Analytics 4, only if you click Accept on the cookie banner. It sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) for up to 13 months and tells Google which pages you view, how long you stay, your device, browser and approximate location. Google Signals and ad personalisation are turned off.

Why: to understand how many people visit, where they come from and what they read, so the site can get better. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)) for Google Analytics; legitimate interest for the cookieless counts.

Who receives it: Google Ireland Ltd and Google LLC (USA) for Google Analytics; Cloudflare for hosting and its analytics. Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which both companies are certified.

How long: Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted. Your cookie choice is remembered for 6 months in your browser, then you are asked again.
