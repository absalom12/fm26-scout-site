FAQ

How do I cancel?

Open the Polar customer portal, sign in with the email you paid with, and press Cancel subscription. You keep the analyst until the end of the period you paid for, and the free app is untouched.

What happens to my key if I stop?

The key stops working at the end of the paid period. The analyst bar goes back to the compact locked line, and everything else in ScoutSheet carries on as before.

Why is this one feature paid?

Each search calls an AI model that bills per use. ScoutSheet is free and open source, and supporters are what pay for the analyst and keep the project going.

Lost the email?

Open your Polar customer portal (also linked in every Polar receipt) and your key is under your order.
